Core Principles

01 / Lock Product Geometry
Keep physical chassis contours, buttons, and brand marks faithful before tuning mood or visual atmosphere.
02 / Track Every Dependency
Document exact model filenames, custom-node versions, inputs, outputs, and seed parameters without undocumented assumptions.
03 / Define Acceptance Gates
Audit every shot for visual identity, temporal stability, artifacts, and commercial readiness.
04 / Preserve Rollback Paths
Keep known-good baseline graphs on hand so any regression from node or model updates can revert immediately.

Delivery Acceptance Checklist

  • Product Silhouette: Body proportions and chassis geometry remain stable across the entire clip with zero structural drift.
  • Hardware Details: Camera islands, side keys, ports, and logos stay strictly faithful to studio reference shots.
  • Temporal Continuity: Free from frame-to-frame flicker, texture crawling, duplicated parts, or unnatural warping.
  • Camera Blocking: Motion matches storyboard intent without obscuring key product selling points.
  • Delivery Specs: Output resolution, frame rate, duration, and codec meet commercial delivery requirements.
  • Asset Archival: Model dependencies, key seeds, and source inputs are archived for reproducible re-renders.
