02 / WHAT I DO

From the first idea to the final detail.

A multidisciplinary practice across content, software and infrastructure. Making things work better and feel considered.

01 / CREATIVE

Video & Visual Production

Commercial video planning, creative direction, scripting, product filming and editing. From product features to campaign stories, the goal is to communicate the value clearly and make every shot count.

02 / EMERGING TOOLS

AI-Assisted Workflows

Building practical image and video workflows with generative tools, ComfyUI, image-to-video techniques and video enhancement — with particular attention to product consistency and repeatability.

03 / DIGITAL

Web & Product Building

Designing and developing lightweight websites, personal tools and focused digital experiences. I care about responsive layouts, useful interactions, clear information and maintainable code.

04 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Networks & Infrastructure

Linux servers, VPS environments, IPv4/IPv6 networking, proxy connectivity, Docker-based services and self-hosted tools — with an emphasis on reliability, observability and safe changes.

03 / HOW I WORK

Clear process. Careful execution.

Good outcomes usually come from making the problem clear before choosing the tools.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the goal, audience, constraints and what a successful result should achieve.

STEP 02

Plan

Break the work into a clear structure, choose the right tools and identify dependencies.

STEP 03

Build & Test

Create in focused iterations, check real-world behavior and keep important details consistent.

STEP 04

Refine

Review the result, remove unnecessary complexity and document what matters for future use.
