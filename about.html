05 / HOW I WORK

Clear process.
Careful execution.

Good outcomes usually come from making the problem clear before choosing the tools. I prefer a repeatable process that makes progress visible and keeps quality under control.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the goal, audience, constraints and what a successful result should achieve.

STEP 02

Plan

Break the work into a clear structure, choose the right tools and identify dependencies.

STEP 03

Build & test

Create in focused iterations, check real-world behavior and keep important details consistent.

STEP 04

Refine

Review the result, remove unnecessary complexity and document what matters for future use.

06 / THE APPROACH

Good work is a mix of curiosity, craft and consistency.

I value simple interfaces, thoughtful details and solutions that solve a real problem. Whether shaping a film, refining a website or building a technical workflow, I care about the whole experience — not just how it looks at first glance.

I am especially interested in the space between creative work and engineering: using technology to expand what is possible while keeping the result coherent, practical and reliable.

I also believe a useful system should be understandable after it is built. Clear documentation, maintainable structure and a safe way to recover are part of the finished work.
