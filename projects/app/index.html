Overview

APP.WWZ.iM is a personal navigation hub designed to make useful websites and online tools easier to find. It brings frequently used destinations into one organized interface, reducing the effort of searching across bookmarks and scattered links.

What it offers

  • A central place to browse useful websites and tools
  • A categorized navigation experience
  • A lightweight interface focused on quick access
